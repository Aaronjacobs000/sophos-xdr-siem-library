# Source: Cisco ACI syslog from the APIC and the fabric switches.
# Child of CISCO_ACI_SYSLOG_PARENT: session log records (aaaSessionLR) whose triggers include login
# or logout, to Auth:
#   [login,session][<severity>][subj-[uni/userext/user-<name>]/sess-<id>] [user <name>] From-<address>-client-type-<type>-<result>
# A remote user (one from RADIUS, TACACS+, LDAP or SAML) is uni/userext/remoteuser-<name>. The
# client type is how they signed in, such as REST, ssh or GUI. Session refreshes stay in the parent.
# sess, rec, node, trig, eventType$, sensorType$, hostId$ and eventTimeUsec$ come from the parent.
