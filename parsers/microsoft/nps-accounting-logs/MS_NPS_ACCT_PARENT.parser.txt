# Record-Date and Record-Time (or Timestamp) are the NPS server's local time, with no zone.
# NXLog's RFC 5424 header gives the local offset when the record is read.
# The date follows the server's regional settings: month first in Microsoft's examples
# (10/14/2026), day first on servers set that way (14/10/2026). A part over 12 settles it;
